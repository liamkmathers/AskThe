# What fans ask

Research notes (2026-10-29) on the questions The Iron Snail's audience actually asks, to shape the test set ([EVALUATION](EVALUATION.md)) and answering.

**Sources.** About 250 genuine questions among the top comments on 36 of his videos, read through YouTube's comment pages, plus search-engine snippets of Reddit (r/BuyItForLife, r/malefashionadvice, r/goodyearwelt and others) and Styleforum. Reddit itself refused automated access, so Reddit themes are paraphrased, not quoted. Shares below are rough.

## Question types

| Type | Share | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care, treatment, break-in (soaking STF, waxing Blundstones, waterproofing, suede) | ~22% | Practical, often two or three steps in one comment |
| Sizing and fit ("true to size?", "I'm 33x30 in 502s, what size in these?") | ~18% | Usually relative to another product or the asker's body |
| Comparisons ("3335 vs 875/1907", "Blundstones or Bean Boots?", "Docs vs Solovair") | ~13% | Mostly within products he has reviewed; often "do you prefer…" |
| What he's wearing, where to get it | ~10% | Often tied to a moment in the video; mostly unanswerable from transcripts |
| Value, price, "worth it", quality decline ("Is RL much lower quality now?") | ~9% | More common on Reddit |
| Video requests ("do beanies", "where's Whitesville?") | ~9% | Natural not-covered questions |
| Long-term ("do you still like these?", "how are they holding up?") | ~6% | Tests newest-verdict-wins directly |
| Suitability ("good for winter?", "4-day hike in Blundstones?", "8 hours comfortable?") | ~6% | Main source of questions that name no product |
| His own products (Mammoth jacket, jeans, collaborations) | ~5% | Answered as his opinion (D14) |
| Personal life (partner, home, height, hair) | ~5% | Never answered (rule 8) |

## What this means for the product

- **Nicknames, model numbers and misspellings are normal:** Blunnies, Docs, Bean Boots, Iron Rangers, Plastagonia, LVC, RL, 501s, STF, 3335s/875s/1907s, 585, W31, 33x30; "Solovairs", "weldt", "tru size". Captions are no better ("bluntstones", "blonde stones"). Name expansion through the glossary and number-aware keyword search carry most of the weight.
- **Many questions are about the asker** ("I'm 5'11, 225 lbs…", "I'm a UK 8"). Answers give his stated advice and say what he didn't cover, never an invented conversion.
- **About a quarter are multi-part.** Answers should address each part or say which part isn't covered.
- **Fans want a decisive pick** ("just tell me which to buy"): supports leading with his verdict (rule 0).
- **Time matters:** "as of late 2023", "still like them?". Answers date his views and lead with the newest.
- **Prices change** ($30 vs $72 for the same jeans), so prices are reported with the video's date.
- **Sponsor reads are frequent and noticed by viewers;** they must never read as his verdict (rule 6).
- **Timestamp questions** ("what jacket at 1:08?") are out of scope (D12).

## For the test set

Include, beyond the mix in EVALUATION: a nickname or caption-misspelt name; a sizing question relative to another product; a multi-part care question; a "still like it?" question with a later video; a question that names no product; a request for an uncovered brand; and a personal-life question that must be declined.
